# Two-minute demo

Record the screen at the laptop, with the altar full-screen in a browser and the terminal out of the way. Aim for about two minutes. Speak the left column. Do the right column while you talk, not after.

Warm Gemma once before you hit record (`ollama pull gemma2:2b`, then send one short story and wait). The first call on a CPU is the long one. If Ollama is not ready, record with `OFRENDA_MOCK_LLM=1` and say so in one sentence. The altar is the same. Only the storyteller changes.

If you can, sit where the webcam sees you. That is the shot. If the camera will not open, ghost mode still places them in a dark room, and you can say that the frame never had to leave the machine either way.

Start from the fictional sample. If you have been experimenting, use **Restore the fictional sample** first.

| Time | Say | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | This is Ofrenda. Remember me. It is an altar I run on my own laptop, for family stories I don't want to lose. | Show the whole page: the name, the paper, the shelves. Point at the status line so it is clear Gemma is local, or that you are on the mock storyteller. |
| 0:15 | The family here is invented. I didn't put my own relatives in a public repo. The Adeyemis are so anyone can try it. The oldest sit highest. Seyi, at the bottom, is the keeper. When a story says "my", it means Seyi. | Move the pointer down the shelves: Ireti, then Ayo and Folake and Tunde, then Funke, Kola, Bisi, then Seyi. |
| 0:35 | Tunde moved to Ghana. The altar has his name and almost nothing else. It will not invent a wife for him. It lights a candle and tells me who to ask. | Scroll to the candles if they are below the fold. Read Tunde's card out loud, including the part about asking Funke. |
| 0:55 | So I ask, and I come back with one sentence. | Click the story box. Type, or paste: `Tunde had a wife called Ama from Accra.` Click **Set them on the altar**. If Gemma is thinking, keep talking: the story stays on this machine, the model is just reading it. |
| 1:15 | Ama is on the shelf beside him. The candle for that gap goes out. The other candles stay, because those questions are still open. Mama Ireti's spouse. Folake's parents. Kola's parents. | Show Ama's frame next to Tunde. Show the candle row without Tunde's card. |
| 1:30 | A name is not a memory. Funke has one. She still ties her gele the way Folake taught her. | Click Funke. Let the side panel show the memory and the photograph. |
| 1:40 | And if I have a photograph, I can sit with her. The cut-out happens here. MediaPipe, on this computer. The camera picture is not uploaded. | Click **Sit with them**. Allow the camera. Let the ghost float for a few seconds with the memory card readable. If you are in frame, don't talk over the first second of it. |
| 1:55 | They only come forward when someone remembers to ask. That is the whole thing. The file stays in the `data` folder. When I close the laptop, it is still mine. | Click Close. End on the altar, not on the desktop. |

If you only have ninety seconds, skip the manual add and skip Kola's candle. Do not skip the ghost.

After recording, put the GIF or video link in `docs/DEV_POST.md` where the placeholder is, and in the README if you want it beside the stills.
